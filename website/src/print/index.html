Print a paper map

For when the internet or GPS is down. Print it now and keep it with a compass.

    Layout

    Printing tips
    • Print at actual size (100%), not "fit to page", so the scale is true.
    • The top of every page is north. Each grid square is the size shown under the map.
    • Numbers at the page edges tell you which page continues the map.
    • Keep a copy in the car and one at home. Paper never runs out of battery.

    Move the map to place the frame. Zoom out to see all of it.
