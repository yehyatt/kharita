Terms and privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026

These terms cover the hosted Mapeleven service at mapeleven.dev: map styles, tiles, fonts, search and accounts. The Mapeleven SDKs and server software are open source under the MIT licence; you can run your own server under that licence instead, and these terms then do not apply to it.

Using the service

  • The service is free. Each account has a monthly allowance, shown on your account page. Requests above it are refused until the next month.
  • Without a key, the service is for development and trying things out, and searches are limited per visitor.
  • Show the map data credit wherever the map appears: "© OpenStreetMap contributors". The SDKs keep it on; do not hide it.
  • Do not bulk-download or copy tiles or search results, resell the service, or try to get around the limits. Keys used that way may be suspended.
  • The service comes as is, with no uptime guarantee. Limits and features may change; we will post notice on this site first. For guaranteed capacity, run your own server.

What we store

  • Your account: your GitHub user ID, user name, display name and profile picture link, from GitHub sign-in. We do not ask GitHub for your email or for access to your repositories.
  • Your keys: their names, the websites you allow, and when they were created or deleted.
  • Usage: the number of map loads, searches and tile requests per key per day.
  • One cookie that keeps you signed in for 30 days. No advertising or tracking cookies, and no analytics scripts.

We do not store your users' locations or search text. Search requests are answered and not logged. IP addresses are held in memory for one minute to apply the per-visitor limit, and are not written to disk.

The service runs on servers at Hetzner in Germany. We do not sell or share your data. To delete your account and everything above, write to hello@mapeleven.dev.

Map data

Map data comes from OpenStreetMap under the Open Database Licence, with the tile schema from Protomaps, and extra places from Overture Maps (Apache 2.0 and CDLA Permissive 2.0). It may be incomplete or out of date; do not rely on it where errors could cause harm. Suggestions sent from the Explorer app go to OpenStreetMap as public notes.
